CHAPTER 01WHO WE ARE

ABOUT ECO-SOCIAL CLUB

The Eco-Social Club (ESC) of Silicon University, Odisha is a student-led initiative committed to advancing environmental sustainability, social responsibility and individual well-being. Guided by the principles of collective action and responsible engagement, the club works towards addressing contemporary environmental and societal challenges through awareness campaigns, outreach programmes and impactful initiatives.

CHAPTER 02ECOLOGY · SOCIAL WELFARE · ZEAL

IDEAS INTO MEANINGFUL ACTION

Operating across three core domains, Ecology, Social Welfare and Zeal, ESC provides a platform for students to translate ideas into meaningful action. Through diverse initiatives, collaborative engagements and community-oriented programmes, the club promotes environmental consciousness, social development and holistic well-being. Its flagship annual event, URVION, has emerged as a prominent platform for sustainability-driven activities and innovation, attracting over 400 participants. Building on this success, URVION 2.0, themed "The Pirates of Caribbean," aims to further expand opportunities for innovation, collaboration and collective impact.

CHAPTER 03OUR COMMUNITY & VISION

A COMMUNITY FOR CHANGE

With a growing community of 500+ registered members and outreach extending beyond its immediate membership, ESC continues to strengthen its presence through meaningful initiatives and strategic collaborations. The club aspires to cultivate socially conscious, environmentally responsible and proactive individuals who contribute towards a healthier, more inclusive and sustainable future.

A few useful details for prospective participants. More event-specific information will be shared soon.

When will the event schedule be announced? −
The festival takes place on 15 and 16 January 2027. Detailed schedules for each challenge will be announced through the official URVION channels.
What events are part of URVION VOLUME 2? +
URVION VOLUME 2 is the Eco-Social Club's Pirates of the Caribbean-themed sustainability festival at Silicon University. It features four signature challenges: ECO-HACK, Green Ideathon, War for Survival and Structura.
Where can I follow updates from the organisers? +
Follow the Eco-Social Club on Instagram at @ecosocialclub_sitb for announcements, updates and upcoming information.
Who can participate? +
Participation details and eligibility will be included in the individual event registration forms and official announcements.
How do I register for an event? +
Each event has its own registration form. Please use the relevant event registration button and review the form carefully before submitting.
Will rules and event details be available? +
Yes. Detailed rules, reporting details and final schedules will be published before the event through official channels.
Where is URVION VOLUME 2 organised? +
URVION VOLUME 2 is organised by the Eco-Social Club at Silicon University, Bhubaneswar.
